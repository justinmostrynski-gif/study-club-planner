STUDY CLUB DEPTH FLOW: READ ME FIRST
====================================

WHAT THIS IS
A planner that walks you from a study club idea to a deeper,
stakeholder-informed design. It is one file. Nothing to install.


HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Move "Study-Club-Depth-Flow.html" to a folder you'll keep,
   for example Documents\Study Club Planner.
2. Double-click it. It opens in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
   (If Windows asks how to open it, choose Microsoft Edge.)
3. Bookmark the page so you can get back to it easily.

Always open the same file from the same place. If you move or
rename it, your browser may not show your saved study clubs.
(You can bring them back by importing your project files.)


YOUR WORK SAVES AUTOMATICALLY
Everything saves in your browser as you type. Look for
"Saved" in the top right corner.

Your work lives in this browser on this computer. To be safe,
export your project files now and then (see below).
Clearing your browser's history and site data can erase it.


SHARING YOUR PROGRESS
Inside a study club, click "Export file". Save the
.depthflow.json file to your team's shared Teams or OneDrive
folder. Your lead imports these files on the "Team progress"
page to see where every study club stands.

To move your work to another computer: export the file there,
then open the planner on the new computer and click
"Import project files".


SHARING THE FINISHED DESIGN
Open the Blueprint (gold button, top right of any study club).
- "Download Word" makes a .docx you can edit and send.
- "Print or save as PDF" opens your printer window. Choose
  "Save as PDF" as the printer to make a PDF.


THE FLOW
01  Define the destination   Start with the behavior, not the topic
02  Ask the roads            Interview your partners with five questions
03  Prioritize few           Find the one or two missing roads
04  Design for depth         Turn each priority road into design decisions
 ✓  Prove + sustain          Measure it and reinforce it

Design delivers the content. Galvanization creates the depth around it.
